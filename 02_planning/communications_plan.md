# Communications Plan
## AML Transaction Monitoring System Implementation

| Communication | Audience | Purpose | Frequency | Channel | Owner |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Steering Committee | CCO, CRO, Head of IT, AML Ops Manager | Decisions, escalations, milestone approval | Monthly | Meeting with status pack | Project Manager |
| Sponsor 1:1 | CCO | Progress, risks, early warnings | Weekly | 30 min meeting | Project Manager |
| Status report | Steering Committee, Internal Audit | RAG status, progress, risks, budget | Every sprint (2 weeks) | Confluence page and email | Project Manager |
| Sprint review | Product Owner, analysts' super users, vendor, IT | Demo working features, collect feedback | Every sprint | Video demo session | Vendor and Product Owner |
| Sprint planning and retrospective | Delivery team | Plan next sprint, improve the way we work | Every sprint | Meeting, Jira board | Project Manager (as Scrum Master) |
| Daily stand up | Delivery team | Progress and blockers | Daily, 15 min | Video call | Project Manager |
| Technical sync | Head of IT, Integration Lead, vendor | Integration and environment issues | Weekly | Meeting | IT Integration Lead |
| Analyst update | All AML analysts and team leads | What is coming, training dates, what changes for them | Monthly | Team meeting and email | AML Ops Manager |
| Branch newsletter item | Branch managers | Awareness of new process | Monthly, then before go live | Internal newsletter | Project Manager |
| Change requests | Change Control Board | Decide scope, cost or schedule changes | As needed | Jira change request and meeting | Project Manager |
| Go live announcement | All staff in scope | Go live date, support contacts | Once | Email and intranet | Sponsor |

## Escalation path

Team member → Project Manager (resolve within 2 business days) → Sponsor (within 5 business days) → Steering Committee.

## Where things live

* **Jira:** backlog, sprints, defects, change requests
* **Confluence:** all project documents, meeting notes, status reports
* **Shared mailbox:** vendor correspondence
