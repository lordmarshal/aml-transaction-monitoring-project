# Project Closure Report
## AML Transaction Monitoring System Implementation

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Project ID | PTB-COMP-2025-01 |
| Sponsor | Linda Okafor, Chief Compliance Officer |
| Project Manager | Blessing Adeniyi, PMP |
| Closure date | December 19, 2025 |

## 1. Summary

The project replaced manual, sample based AML transaction reviews with an automated monitoring system. It went live on **November 10, 2025** as planned, monitors **100%** of transactions including Interac e-Transfers, and was delivered for **$1,705,000**, which is **$95,000 (5.3%) under** the approved $1.8 million budget. Every milestone was met on its baseline date.

## 2. Objectives: planned vs achieved

| Objective | Target | Result at closure | Met? |
|---|---|---|---|
| Transaction coverage | 100% by Nov 10, 2025 | 100% from Nov 10, 2025 (about 1.38 million transactions a month including e-Transfers) | ✔ |
| Budget | Within $1.8M | $1,705,000 | ✔ |
| Alert triage time | 5 business days or less | 3.1 business days average (first 6 weeks) | ✔ |
| LCTRs on time | 100% within 15 calendar days | 100% (142 of 142) | ✔ |
| STRs | Submitted as soon as practicable | 11 STRs submitted, all within the bank's internal timeline | ✔ |
| Training | 24 analysts and 3 team leads | 27 of 27 trained and passed | ✔ |
| False positive rate | Below 85% | 82% | ✔ |

**Before vs after:** about 60,000 transactions reviewed a month (5%) → about 1.38 million screened a month (100%).
